## The Challenges

### Challenge 1: Daily Inspirational Quote Generator

**Difficulty:** Beginner

**The brief:**
Build an interactive Artifact that displays a beautifully designed inspirational quote. Each time the user clicks a "New Quote" button, a different quote appears with a different background color/gradient. Include the author's name and a "Copy to Clipboard" button so users can share the quote.

**Requirements:**
- At least 20 pre-loaded quotes (ask Claude to pick good ones)
- Smooth transition animation between quotes
- Visually stunning -- this should look like an Instagram story
- Works on mobile screens
- A "Share" button that copies the quote as formatted text

**You'll use:** Artifact creation, design description, iteration

**Bonus round:** Add a category filter (motivational, funny, philosophical, career) and a "Quote of the Day" feature that shows the same quote all day based on the date.

---

### Challenge 2: PDF Study Notes Generator with Interactive Artifacts

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**The brief:**
Create a two-part workflow:

**Part 1:** Upload a PDF (a textbook chapter, article, or report) to Claude and ask it to generate structured study notes with: key concepts, definitions, a summary, and 5 practice questions.

**Part 2:** Ask Claude to turn those study notes into an interactive Artifact with:
- Tabbed navigation (Concepts | Definitions | Summary | Quiz)
- The Quiz tab has multiple-choice questions with immediate feedback
- A progress tracker showing how many questions you've answered
- A "Score" display at the end
- Clean, study-app aesthetic

**Requirements:**
- Use a real PDF (find a free textbook chapter online or use a work document)
- The quiz questions should be genuinely based on the PDF content
- Include a "Study Again" button that resets the quiz
- Mobile-friendly layout

**You'll use:** File upload, prompt engineering, Artifact creation, multi-step workflow

**Bonus round:** Add a "Generate Flashcards" button that creates a flashcard-style review mode within the same Artifact, with flip-to-reveal functionality.

### Challenge 3: Complete Personal Portfolio Website Deployed to Netlify

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**The brief:**
Build and deploy a complete personal portfolio website that showcases who you are and what you can do. This is a real website on the real internet that you can put on your resume and share on LinkedIn.

**Requirements:**
- **Home section:** Your name, title/tagline, and a brief introduction
- **About section:** A longer bio, your professional background, what drives you
- **Projects section:** Showcase 3+ tools/projects you've built (including ones from this book!)
- **Skills section:** A visual representation of your skills (progress bars, tags, or cards)
- **Contact section:** Links to your LinkedIn, email, and any other professional profiles
- **Design:** Professional, clean, modern -- something you'd be proud to share with a recruiter
- **Responsive:** Must look good on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Deployed:** Live on Netlify with a custom subdomain (yourname.netlify.app)

**Steps:**
1. Ask Claude to build the complete website as an Artifact
2. Iterate on the design until you love it
3. Get the HTML file from Claude
4. Deploy to Netlify Drop (Section 7.7)
5. Claim the site and customize your URL
6. Share the link on LinkedIn with a post about your journey

**You'll use:** Everything from every chapter

**Bonus round:** Add a blog section where you write about your experience learning to build with AI. Your first post: "How I Built This Portfolio Website Without Writing a Single Line of Code."
