> *"A single-conversation Claude is like a goldfish. Projects turns it into your long-term partner. One that actually remembers your name."*

---

Let's talk about the most frustrating thing about working with Claude. Not errors. Not layout bugs. Not the occasional confident wrong answer. No, the most frustrating thing is this:

**Every new conversation, Claude forgets everything.**

You spent 45 minutes in a previous chat explaining your company, your role, your writing style, your project requirements. Claude nailed it. Beautiful output. You were thrilled. Then you came back the next day, started a new conversation, and Claude greeted you like a stranger at a cocktail party. "Hi! How can I help you today?" Zero memory of yesterday. Zero recollection of your company, your style preferences, or the fact that you hate bullet points in executive summaries.

It's like having the world's most talented colleague who gets amnesia every time they leave the office.

This chapter fixes that. **Projects** is Claude's long-term memory system -- a way to create persistent workspaces where Claude remembers who you are, what you're working on, and how you like things done. Across every conversation, every day, every week.

No more re-explaining yourself. No more "as I mentioned in our previous conversation" (spoiler: it doesn't remember previous conversations). No more starting from scratch every single time.
