Here's a dirty secret about Claude that most people miss: **it's not just a chatbot.** It's a personal data analyst, document translator, image interpreter, and spreadsheet wizard all rolled into one -- and all it needs is for you to upload a file and ask a question.

Most people use Claude like a fancy Google search. They type questions, get answers, move on. But the moment you start **feeding it your actual files** -- your spreadsheets, your PDFs, your screenshots, your messy data dumps -- that's when Claude transforms from "interesting toy" into "how did I ever work without this?"

This chapter is about that transformation.

We're going to upload Excel files and get instant analysis. We're going to feed Claude a 50-page report and get a one-page executive summary. We're going to show it a screenshot of a dashboard and ask "what does this mean?" We're going to discover that Claude can run actual code in the cloud to crunch your data. And then we're going to chain it all together into a complete workflow.

If Chapter 3 was about making things you can *see*, Chapter 4 is about making sense of things you already *have*.
