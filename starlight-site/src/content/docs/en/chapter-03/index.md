If Chapter 2 taught you how to *talk* to Claude, this chapter teaches you how to make Claude **show you things**.

Meet **Artifacts** -- Claude's most powerful visual weapon, and honestly the feature that makes most non-developers gasp out loud the first time they see it. Imagine describing a website, a chart, or an interactive tool in plain English... and then *watching it appear right next to your conversation*, clickable and everything.

No installing anything. No "development environment." No Googling "what is a terminal." You type words, and things appear. That's it.

Artifacts is where the "AI magic pen" metaphor stops being a metaphor and starts being literal. You describe, Claude draws. You refine, Claude redraws. It's like having a designer-developer hybrid sitting next to you, except this one never sighs when you say "actually, can we try it in blue?"
