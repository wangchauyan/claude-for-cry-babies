> *"I have a million-dollar idea but zero lines of code. Story of my life."*

---

Let's get something out of the way right now: **you don't need to learn to code.**

You need to learn to **describe what you want clearly**. That's it. That's the whole trick. If you can explain to a waiter exactly how you want your steak cooked, you can tell Claude how to build you a tool.

For decades, there's been a giant wall between "people with ideas" and "people who can build things." On one side: product managers, marketers, teachers, writers, small business owners, and basically everyone with a smartphone and a dream. On the other side: developers, engineers, and that one friend who "does computers."

That wall just got a sledgehammer taken to it. The sledgehammer is called AI.
