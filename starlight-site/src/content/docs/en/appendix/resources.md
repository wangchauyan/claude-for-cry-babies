## Recommended Books

These books shaped the thinking behind "Claude for Cry Babies" and will help you continue growing:

### For the Builder Mindset

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
|------|--------|------------|
| **The Lean Startup** | Eric Ries | The bible of MVP thinking. Where the Wizard of Oz MVP concept comes from. Teaches you to validate ideas with minimal investment -- exactly what you've been doing with Claude. |
| **Sprint** | Jake Knapp | How Google Ventures designs and tests ideas in 5 days. The same "build fast, test fast" mentality you've been practicing. |
| **Show Your Work!** | Austin Kleon | Why sharing your work matters, even when it's imperfect. Short, visual, encouraging. Perfect for overcoming the "it's not ready" fear from Section 7.7. |

### For Better Communication

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Don't Make Me Think** | Steve Krug | The classic book on intuitive design. Will make you better at describing what you want Claude to build, because you'll understand what makes things usable. |
| **Made to Stick** | Chip & Dan Heath | Why some ideas survive and others die. Helps you describe your ideas more compellingly -- to AI and to humans. |
| **On Writing Well** | William Zinsser | Clear writing = clear thinking = clear prompts. This book teaches concision, which is the #1 prompt engineering skill. |

### For Understanding AI

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Co-Intelligence** | Ethan Mollick | The most practical, least hype-y book about working alongside AI. Written by a professor who actually uses these tools daily. |
| **AI 2041** | Kai-Fu Lee & Chen Qiufan | Science fiction stories paired with AI explanations. Entertaining way to understand where AI is heading without reading academic papers. |

## One Last Recommendation

The best resource isn't on this list. It's **Claude itself.**

Whenever you want to learn something new, just ask:

> "I want to learn about [topic]. I'm a complete beginner. Can you give me a 10-minute overview that covers the key concepts, why it matters, and what I should explore next?"

You have a patient, knowledgeable tutor available 24/7. Use it.
