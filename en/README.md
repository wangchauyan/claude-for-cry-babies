# Claude for Cry Babies

## A Non-Developer's Guide to AI-Powered Building

### by CW

---

Have you ever stared at a screen full of code and felt like you were reading an alien language? Or wanted to build a simple automation tool, only to give up at the first step of setting up a development environment?

This book is for you.

We won't teach you to code. We'll teach you how to **tell AI what to build for you**. Starting today, you're no longer just a consumer of technology — you're a builder.

---

### Who is this book for?

- **Primary audience**: Non-technical professionals (PMs, marketers, salespeople, designers, managers)
- **Secondary audience**: People with some technical skills who want to 10x their productivity with AI
- **Common pain point**: Having great ideas but no technical means to bring them to life

### The Running Project: "Cry Baby Weekly Report Generator"

From Chapter 1 to Chapter 8, you'll progressively build a real, usable product: **an interactive tool that turns your weekly work notes into polished reports**. Each chapter evolves the project, and by Chapter 8 it's a complete MVP.

### Tone Guide

This book reads like chatting with a smart friend over drinks — humorous but not cringe. The self-deprecating "cry baby" persona is seasoning, not the main dish. The core is always solid, practical teaching.

Every concept comes with a "plain English translation" + "real-world scenario." No abstract concepts left hanging in the air.

---
