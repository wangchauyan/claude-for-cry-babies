# Chapter 7: Zero to One -- Build Your MVP in a Weekend

> *"Perfection is the enemy of launching. Also the enemy of your weekend. Ship it."*

---

You've learned to talk to Claude. You've built Artifacts. You've analyzed files, fixed errors, organized Projects, and evolved a weekly report generator from a markdown template to an interactive app. You've come a long way, cry baby.

Now it's time to build something **real**. Something you can show someone and say, "Yeah, I made that."

This is the MVP chapter -- where you take everything you've learned and put it together into actual, tangible, *useful* things. Not "useful in theory." Useful as in "my coworker just asked me where they can get one."

But first, let's kill a myth that's been haunting ambitious non-developers since the dawn of Silicon Valley: the idea that a Minimum Viable Product needs to be "minimum" in impact. Nope. It needs to be minimum in *scope* and maximum in *"holy crap, this actually works."*

MVP isn't "make it perfect." It's "make it presentable." There's a universe of difference between those two things, and that universe is where shipping happens.
